Magnetic Detection

3-axis magnetometer reads X, Y, Z field values to detect the presence of buried cables beneath the surface.

GPS Tracking

Every detection is stamped with a GPS coordinate so you can map exactly where cables were found.

Live Dashboard

Supabase Realtime pushes every sensor reading to the dashboard instantly — no page refresh needed.

Remote On/Off

Turn the ESP32 on or off directly from the dashboard. The command is written to Supabase and the device polls it every 5 seconds.

Detection History

Full log of every detection event with timestamp, signal strength, GPS coordinates, and status — exportable as CSV.

Configurable Settings

Adjust device ID, signal thresholds, and poll intervals from the Settings page without touching the firmware.
